Golf Remittance - Account Deletion Request

Developer: One1byte

You can request deletion of your Golf Remittance account and associated app data without opening the app.

How to request deletion

Send an email from the Google account associated with Golf Remittance to:

Request Account Deletion

Email: one1byte1@gmail.com

Use the subject: Golf Remittance Account Deletion Request. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request. Never send your password.

What will be deleted

After account ownership is verified, we will process deletion of your Golf Remittance Firebase Authentication account and any associated app data under our control, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Game history, unfinished games, and exported PDFs are stored locally on your device. You can delete them using the in-app deletion process when available or by clearing the app's storage. Files copied or shared outside the app must be deleted separately.

Data that may be retained

Some transaction records, subscription information, or security records may be retained by Google or other service providers according to their policies and applicable legal requirements. We do not delete your Google account.

Deleting your Golf Remittance account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact ;one1byte1@gmail.com.
